RIGHT TV (screen 3)

Put video files for the right TV in this folder.
- Size: 1920 x 1080
- Format: .mp4 (H.264), 30 fps, under 100 MB each
- Several videos play one after another in name order, then start over.
- One video just loops.
- To change a video, delete or replace the file. The wall picks it up within about 7 minutes.
