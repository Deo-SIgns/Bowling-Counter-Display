MIDDLE TWO TVs (screen 2)

Put the wide video for the two middle TVs in this folder.
- Size: 3840 x 1080 (one video stretched across both TVs)
- Format: .mp4 (H.264), 30 fps, under 100 MB each
- For a long video, cut it into parts named part01.mp4, part02.mp4 and so on. They play back to back in name order.
- To change a video, delete or replace the file. The wall picks it up within about 7 minutes.
